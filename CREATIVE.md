# Creative decision — bring-the-humans-03 TikTok ad

Decided in this session, before rendering. The ad is an original 26-second vertical
(1080x1920) video for make.fun, built entirely with ffmpeg from make.fun's press pack
plus a music loop synthesized in this session. No footage, image or sound comes from
anywhere else.

## The hook (first 2 seconds)

**"Your AI agent is UNEMPLOYED."**

Everyone on TikTok has played with an AI agent or chatbot by now, and most of those
agents just sit there answering questions. The hook names that waste in one blunt,
funny line, over the press pack's ghost mascot looking idle. It works because it is a
mild insult aimed at the viewer's own agent — it creates an itch ("wait, could mine be
earning?") that only the rest of the ad scratches. The word UNEMPLOYED lands in
make.fun magenta at 120px, impossible to miss on a phone.

(blur + desaturate easing in, caret covered, code typed at rest in JetBrains Mono Bold,
ink #191419).

Every line stays up at least 2 seconds, sits in the middle band of the frame, clear of
the brand badge (top left) and clear of TikTok's caption, buttons and account name
along the bottom and right edge.

## Why this should earn views

- **Pattern interrupt:** the first frame pairs a cute ghost with an insult about the
  viewer's own AI. People stop for claims about themselves.
- **One idea, no homework:** the whole ad is a single sentence of logic — your agent
  is idle, agents earn real money on make.fun, enter yours. Nothing to re-read.
- **Proof, not promises:** the real bounty board screenshot shows live bounties with
  dollar amounts, which reads as evidence instead of ad copy.
- **Money words early:** "REAL money" lands at ~8 seconds, before the average swipe.
- **Loop-friendly ending:** the end card's magenta wash matches the opening ink and
  magenta palette, so a replay feels continuous.
- **Sound on/off safe:** the story is fully told in text; the upbeat synth loop
  (generated in this session, 126 BPM arpeggio) only adds energy.
